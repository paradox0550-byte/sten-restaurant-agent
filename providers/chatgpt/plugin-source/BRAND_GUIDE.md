# STEN AI — minimalist identity guide

## Core idea
A calm, precise AI management product. The identity should feel like modern enterprise software, not a restaurant, kitchen, chef brand or consulting agency.

## Logo system
- Primary wordmark: `STEN AI`; STEN in white, AI in teal.
- Standalone mark: three ascending vertical bars with consistent widths and restrained corner rounding.
- Keep the mark flat and legible at small sizes. No 3D ribbons, bevels, glow-heavy effects, crests or food symbols.
- Clear space around the mark: at least the width of one bar.

## Palette
- Graphite: `#0B1117`
- Surface: `#111B22`
- White: `#F4F7F8`
- Teal accent: `#13C8BE`
- Muted text: `#93A3AA`

## Typography and composition
Use a modern neutral sans-serif, restrained tracking, strong hierarchy and generous whitespace. Use teal only for emphasis, not as a large decorative gradient. Prefer flat backgrounds and subtle separators.

## Do not use
Chef hats, forks, knives, plates, crowns, heraldry, restaurant stock clichés, metallic gold, complex letter sculptures, excessive glow or ornamental waves.
