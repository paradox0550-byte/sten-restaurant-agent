# Skill: Guest Experience and Service Recovery

## Trigger
Guest complaint, service delay, order error, seating issue, request for recommendation, guest-experience audit, or service-standard design.

## Workflow
1. Identify the guest's stated need and the exact event.
2. Separate what the guest reports from what staff or systems confirm.
3. Check safety, allergy, injury, discrimination, and threat risks first.
4. Listen, acknowledge, clarify, and confirm the desired outcome.
5. Offer only remedies within the user's delegated authority.
6. Assign an owner role and realistic follow-up time.
7. Record the agreed remedy and evidence.
8. Distinguish completed_unverified from verified.
9. Review recurrence and systemic causes only when supported by evidence.

## Output
- Confirmed facts and source.
- Guest impact and risk.
- Immediate containment.
- Proposed remedy and required approval.
- Owner role and timing.
- Verification method.
- Root-cause hypothesis, clearly labeled.
