# STEN Knowledge Tree

## 01. Restaurant economics
P&L architecture; chart of accounts; revenue bridge; plan/fact; controllable and non-controllable expenses; EBITDA and cash discipline; unit economics; scenario analysis.

## 02. Product cost and cost control
COGS; kitchen and bar cost; recipe costing; yield; portion control; purchasing price variance; waste; stocktaking; Food Cost; Labor Cost; Prime Cost.

## 03. Workforce and productivity
Role passports; staffing model; schedules; timekeeping; payroll models; advances; overtime; productivity; labor forecasting; training and retention.

## 04. Menu and commercial performance
ABC by explicitly selected basis; contribution margin; menu engineering; pricing architecture; menu descriptions; choice architecture; seasonality; channel mix; menu tests.

## 05. Operations and shift management
Pre-shift briefing; readiness; opening; service coordination; stop-list; handover; close; checklists; escalation; event/task lifecycle.

## 06. Guest service, hospitality and etiquette
Arrival; seating; menu orientation; needs discovery; recommendation; order confirmation; pacing; serving; check-back; bill; farewell; complaints; recovery; accessibility; cultural etiquette.

## 07. Guest psychology and consumer behavior
Perceived value; expectations; trust; decision effort; choice overload; social context; sensory experience; fairness; loyalty; behavioral research limitations.

## 08. Leadership and people
Leadership; feedback; coaching; onboarding; psychological safety; emotional labor; conflict; motivation; workload; retention; fair performance management.

## 09. Audit, risk and execution
Audit planning; evidence quality; food safety escalation; accountability; SLA; corrective action; verification; recurrence; root-cause analysis.

## 10. Trends and development
AI and automation; omnichannel; reservation/discovery; personalization; value-seeking behavior; sustainability; menu innovation; labor and cost pressure; format evolution.

Each topic must be versioned and labeled CORE, RESEARCH, INDUSTRY, TREND, HYPOTHESIS, or CONFIGURATION.
