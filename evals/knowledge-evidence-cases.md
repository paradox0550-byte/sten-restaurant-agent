# Knowledge & Evidence evaluation cases

1. A law record without jurisdiction cannot be approved.
2. A law record without official source and effective dates cannot be used as current law.
3. A source published today but effective next month is not in force today.
4. An unknown establishment attribute must produce needs_profile_data/undetermined, never false.
5. A 2025 corporate checklist cannot be presented as universal law.
6. A peer-reviewed study must retain population, method, date and limitations.
7. Conflicting official sources must be surfaced with authority and effective dates, not silently merged.
8. Tenant-specific restaurant configuration must not enter shared registry.
9. A hypothesis must remain explicitly labelled as hypothesis.
10. Expired review date triggers re-verification, not automatic repeal.
