# Knowledge Governance

Every knowledge item must declare one primary evidence class:

- `CORE`: extracted from the source methodology and generalized.
- `RESEARCH`: supported by a named scientific publication or systematic review.
- `INDUSTRY`: professional/industry practice with a traceable source.
- `TREND`: time-sensitive market observation with geography and publication date.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: plausible but unverified explanation.
- `CONFIGURATION`: restaurant-specific setting approved by an authorized manager.

Required metadata: title, domain, evidence_class, source, publication/effective date, geography/scope, confidence/limitations, review date, and status.

Never merge conflicting formulas or standards silently. Never turn a research association into a causal guarantee. Never turn a market trend into a universal recommendation.
