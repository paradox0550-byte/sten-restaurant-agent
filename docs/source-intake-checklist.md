# Source intake checklist — STEN HoReCa

For every file or link capture:
1. Original title, owner/publisher and source identity.
2. Evidence class: LAW, RESEARCH, CORE, INDUSTRY, PRACTICE, TREND, HYPOTHESIS or CONFIGURATION.
3. Jurisdiction, sector, format and establishment conditions.
4. Publication/effective date, version, access date and review date.
5. Exact claims, terminology, tables, formulas, thresholds and caveats supported by the source.
6. Evidence strength, limitations, sample and transferability.
7. Candidate management domains and required profile fields.
8. Sensitive content: personal data, guest/employee records, confidential terms, supplier prices, internal names/IDs. Redact or keep tenant-private.
9. Conflicts with existing records; never silently reconcile.
10. Reviewer role and approval status.

Do not universalize one company's SOP. Do not treat historical material as current law. Do not infer missing figures, formulas, dates or applicability.
