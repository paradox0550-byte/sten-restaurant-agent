# Private Research Vault and Product Knowledge Boundary

## Purpose

STEN is an original intellectual product. User-provided materials are confidential research inputs and expert context, not a corpus to publish, redistribute or expose to tenants. Product methods must be independently reasoned, authored and validated.

## Two distinct knowledge planes

### Restricted research vault
Stores original files, source identities, authors, organizations, correspondence, file hashes, exact source anchors, permissions, confidentiality terms, expert annotations and review history. It is used only by authorized research, audit and governance workflows.

### Product knowledge plane
Stores STEN-authored methods, decision logic, calculations, procedures, controls and explanatory content. It must not contain source filenames, titles, authors, organizations, private IDs, correspondence IDs, private file locators, copied wording, copied tables or customer-specific data.

The research vault is not a retrieval source for ordinary tenant conversations. Only a reviewed and approved derived method may move into the product plane.

## Expert input handling

User-provided expertise must be captured as structured propositions:
- operating problem and desired outcome;
- observed condition and context;
- decision rule or managerial judgment;
- exception, risk and boundary;
- confidence and validation status;
- relevant restaurant format, scale and jurisdiction.

Do not treat every supplied statement as universally true. Preserve the user's assessment and scope, identify hypotheses separately, and ask for clarification when a critical premise is ambiguous.

## Independent method development

For each method:
1. Define the problem and measurable outcome.
2. Extract expert insights without copying source expression.
3. Conduct targeted international research using primary standards, official guidance and peer-reviewed evidence where relevant.
4. Compare approaches, assumptions, contexts and limitations.
5. Design a STEN-native method with its own terminology, sequence, decisions, roles, measures, exception paths and feedback loop.
6. Verify deterministic calculations independently.
7. Record what is established, inferred, configurable or unknown.
8. Obtain specialist review and pilot evidence where the risk warrants it.
9. Release only the STEN-authored product formulation after approval.

Changing names or paraphrasing sentence-by-sentence is not sufficient to make a method original. The method must reflect a genuine synthesis and add coherent operational logic.

## Research hierarchy

Use sources according to the claim:
- current official legislation and regulator guidance for legal obligations;
- recognized international standards for management-system architecture;
- peer-reviewed systematic reviews and primary studies for empirical claims;
- professional industry research for comparative practice;
- user experience and field observations for context-specific operational insight.

Distinguish mandatory rules, standards, research findings, professional practice, user expertise, hypotheses and local configuration. Never convert one evidence class into another.

## Tenant and confidentiality controls

- Never expose the research vault to tenants.
- Never place another tenant's files, operational metrics, staff information or manager preferences into a tenant's retrieval context.
- Never include private source names or provenance in user exports, prompts, generated SOPs or knowledge-pack attachments.
- Public citations may be provided when a user asks for substantiation of a public factual or legal claim; they must not reveal private user materials.
- Apply access controls, retention rules and rights review before storing or reusing third-party or confidential material.

## Release gate

A product pack is releasable only when:
- private-source scan passes;
- source registries and correspondence metadata are excluded;
- every method is STEN-authored and within its approval state;
- tenant isolation checks pass;
- content, calculation and answer-quality tests pass;
- release approval is recorded.
