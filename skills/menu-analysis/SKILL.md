# Skill: Menu Engineering

1. Confirm restaurant, menu version, period, channel, and item identifiers.
2. Reconcile POS quantities and revenue with source totals.
3. Validate discounts, refunds, voids, comps, and tax treatment.
4. Confirm current recipe version and variable cost.
5. Calculate contribution only when price and cost inputs are complete.
6. Ask the operator to select ABC basis: quantity, revenue, or contribution.
7. Require approved cumulative A/B/C thresholds.
8. Require approved popularity and contribution cutoffs for the menu matrix.
9. Segment by daypart/channel only when sample size and source quality support it.
10. Recommend controlled tests; define a measurement period and success metric.

Never classify on missing cost data as if margin were zero. Do not treat menu design research as a guaranteed sales lift.
