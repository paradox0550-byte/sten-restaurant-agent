# Financial analysis workflow

1. Confirm establishment, legal entity, department, currency, tax basis, period and source system.
2. Identify actual/plan/forecast and reconcile source totals.
3. Validate account mapping and formula definitions before calculating ratios.
4. Separate revenue, discounts, refunds, taxes, COGS, labor, controllable and non-controllable expenses, capex and financing items.
5. Calculate only with complete, compatible inputs. Preserve numerator, denominator, unit, period and source lineage.
6. Compare against approved restaurant-specific targets only; historical values are not universal benchmarks.
7. Explain material variances, separate facts from hypotheses and quantify impact only when inputs support it.
8. Recommend owner, timing and verification metric.

Never infer missing lines as zero or silently substitute a denominator. Refer to `knowledge/finance/`.
