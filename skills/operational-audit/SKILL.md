# Operational audit workflow

1. Define the establishment, zone, shift, date and audit scope.
2. Identify the approved standard and its version.
3. Collect observations and evidence; distinguish observation from hearsay.
4. Classify finding severity using approved local criteria; do not invent thresholds.
5. Assign an owner role, deadline and required corrective action.
6. Verify completion with evidence; a checked box or verbal assurance alone may not prove completion.
7. Record recurrence and unresolved risks.
8. Escalate immediate food, guest, staff or fire-safety risks through the approved local procedure.

See `knowledge/restaurant-operations/` and `knowledge/service-standards/`.
