# Research and Industry Evidence Register

**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-14  
**Rule:** Research informs questions and experiments; it does not automatically create a restaurant SOP or universal KPI.

## Menu design and consumer behavior
- `RESEARCH`: The effect of menu design on consumer behavior: A meta-analysis. International Journal of Hospitality Management, 108 (2023), 103353. 53 studies, n=16,522. Effects vary by design dimension; larger effects were found for physiological measures, moderate effects for intentions/attitudes, and marginal effects for actual purchase. Laboratory/mock-menu effects may exceed field/real-menu effects. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhm.2022.103353
- `RESEARCH`: The compass for restaurant menu design experiments: TCCM review of 85 papers. International Journal of Hospitality Management (2025), 104118. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhm.2025.104118
- `RESEARCH`: Menu Design: A Review of Literature. Journal of Foodservice Business Research (2015), 18(3), 189–206. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/15378020.2015.1051428

## Service quality and guest experience
- `RESEARCH`: A quarter century of studies on customer experience in restaurants: systematic literature review of 135 peer-reviewed articles (2026). Identifies food experience, experiencescape, and consumer profile as antecedents; outcomes include perceived value, satisfaction, delight, loyalty, brand love, place attachment, and well-being. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/TQM-04-2025-0260
- `RESEARCH`: DINESERV and restaurant service quality: systematic review of 30 articles, published online 21 Aug 2026. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/10686967.2026.2714503
- `RESEARCH`: Integrative review of QSR service-quality research, 108 studies (2026). Finds no consensus on one best scale and notes that technology-mediated encounters remain under-measured. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhm.2025.104367

## Industry context and trends (US-centric unless otherwise stated)
- `INDUSTRY/TREND`: National Restaurant Association, State of the Restaurant Industry 2026, published 11 Feb 2026. Projects US sales and employment growth while emphasizing cost pressures and technology investment. https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/research-reports/state-of-the-industry
- `INDUSTRY/TREND`: McKinsey, What US consumers want from restaurants in 2026. Uses transaction data across 18+ markets and a survey of roughly 900 US consumers (Aug 2025); discusses value sensitivity, channel mix, AI/digital agents, and menu/occasion shifts. https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-insights/what-us-consumers-want-from-restaurants-in-2026
- `INDUSTRY/TREND`: National Restaurant Association connected technology research (2026). Reports adoption, operator concerns, productivity expectations, and the continuing importance of hospitality. https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/media/press-releases/new-research-from-the-national-restaurant-association-identifies-connected-technology-as-restaurants/
- `INDUSTRY/TREND`: Toast restaurant dining survey, published 12 Nov 2026; US survey of 850 diners. Treat as vendor survey, not universal population evidence. https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/data/restaurant-trends

## Use limitations
- US findings are not automatically transferable to Europe or other markets.
- Vendor surveys can have sampling and commercial biases.
- Cross-sectional associations do not prove causality.
- A trend must include date, geography, sample, and source; re-check before using it for a current decision.
- Do not quote a percentage without preserving its denominator and research context.
